# SPEC-045: Binding capstone — provenance, determinism, and hardening

> **OUTLINE — `cycle: frame`.** This spec exists so its ID is stable and
> siblings can declare `depends_on: [SPEC-045]`. Capture **scope** (Context /
> Goal / Non-Goals) and **dependencies** only — the *approach* is designed
> just-in-time when this moves to `design`. Do not pre-design it here.

## Context

The capstone of `STAGE-011` (`PROJ-003`): once the Compression-7 binding exists
(SPEC-043) and is proven bit-exact (SPEC-044), three closing obligations remain —
a provenance-ledger entry for the DNG Compression-7 **binding** (distinct from
STAGE-009's T.81 Annex H **decode** row), a determinism assertion that the same
compressed DNG yields byte-identical plane output on macOS and Linux, and
hardening of the container→decoder boundary against truncated / hostile
Compression-7 strips. With it, STAGE-011 and PROJ-003 close. The dev-only
`fuzzing` feature + `__fuzz_ljpeg` hook from SPEC-038 may be retired here once the
real public Compression-7 entry exists (DEC-036 Validation).

## Goal

Add a provenance-ledger entry for the DNG Compression-7 binding, assert
byte-identical plane output across macOS and Linux for a held Compression-7 file,
and harden the container→decoder boundary against truncated / hostile strips
(typed `Error`, never a panic) — closing STAGE-011 and PROJ-003.
